# zh
## 專案簡介

為 2016 年台北亮點設計製作的夜市專用環保袋，將碗與提袋結合，使用後可摺疊收納，一份包裝可容納 3 到 5 個。材質選用石頭紙與食品級矽膠。

# en
## Overview

A reusable night-market bag designed in 2016. It combines a bowl and a carrier bag, folds away for storage and holds 3 to 5 bags in one pack. The materials are stone paper and food-grade silicone.
