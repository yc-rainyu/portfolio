# zh
## 專案簡介

與光創意數位印刷合作，完成企業識別設計，從同業分析與標誌草圖開始，以 10 個標誌方案進行問卷調查，再完成設計定稿與識別應用。

# en
## Overview

A corporate identity project in collaboration with Sunlight Digital Printing. The process covered competitor analysis, logo sketches, a survey comparing 10 logo proposals, and the final identity design and its applications.
